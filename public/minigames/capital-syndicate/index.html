Operation Ferrum Wings

Clear four sectors and break the blockade. Escort the freighter. Come back for what matters.

Mission Briefing

  • Survive the gauntlet. Destroy hostiles and dodge fire — ships that slip past the bottom simply leave.
  • Ram them. Your hull is a weapon. Collisions hurt both ships.
  • Conserve ammo. Gun is endless. Ion is a slow heavy pierce shot. Plasma is a short spread. Rockets (4) fire one smart missile with a wide blast.
  • Super laser. Kills charge the laser (5 / normal, 20 / boss). At 100%, press Space to fire.
  • Strategic suicide. Press 0 twice once per sector to eject half your ammo and clear nearby hostiles.
  • Power-ups. Grab shield / repair / super shield / rockets — or let them pass for score (super shield = extra life).
  • Four sectors. Bosses guard sectors 1–3. Sector 4 ends with a three-phase final commander.
