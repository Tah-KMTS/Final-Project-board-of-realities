# Yu-Gi-Oh! reference — for Yu-Gi-Oh/Domino City (worlds 3 & 4) redesign

Scope: **only the original Yu-Gi-Oh! era where Yugi Muto is the protagonist** (original manga + Duel Monsters anime). Deliberately excludes GX, 5D's, Zexal, Arc-V, VRAINS, SEVENS and their casts/mechanics (Synchro/Xyz/Pendulum/Link summoning, Academy setting, etc. are all out of scope). Summary in our own words for design reference, not reproduced text.

## World-building

- **Domino City**: the setting - a modern Japanese city that hosts the game's major dueling tournaments and is home to KaibaCorp's headquarters. This game's existing "Domino City" world/scene name is already correctly placed.
- **Duel Monsters**: the in-universe card game; duelists use Monster, Spell (called "Magic" in the original), and Trap cards, each duelist starting at 8000 Life Points (already reflected in this game's 8000-LP duel scale).
- **KaibaCorp**: Seto Kaiba's technology conglomerate, manufactures dueling tech (including the holographic projectors used in tournaments) and bankrolls major tournaments.
- **Millennium Items**: seven ancient Egyptian magical artifacts (Puzzle, Rod, Eye, Ring, Key, Scale, Necklace), each granting its bearer a distinct supernatural power; central to the series' mystical/Egyptian-mythology backstory.
- **Shadow Games**: high-stakes mystical duels (often invoking Millennium Item power) where losing carries a real supernatural penalty, not just an in-game one - the tonal core of "this card game has real stakes" that separates early-series stories from purely-sport-focused later arcs.
- **The Pharaoh's spirit**: an ancient Egyptian pharaoh's soul sealed in the Millennium Puzzle for 3000 years, who shares Yugi's body and takes over during high-stakes duels; his own lost memories/identity (later revealed as Pharaoh Atem) are a major throughline.
- **Egyptian God cards**: three unique, extremely powerful cards (Slifer the Sky Dragon, Obelisk the Tormentor, The Winged Dragon of Ra) tied directly to the Pharaoh's backstory - a good template for a small set of "mythic-tier" cards above the normal power curve.

## Major characters

- **Yugi Muto** — protagonist, solves the ancient Millennium Puzzle at the story's start, shares his body/mind with the Pharaoh's spirit. Kind, principled, cares more about the "heart of the cards" than winning at any cost.
- **Yami Yugi / Pharaoh Atem** — the ancient spirit sharing Yugi's body, takes over during serious duels, more ruthless/confident than Yugi himself, driven by amnesia about his own past identity.
- **Seto Kaiba** — KaibaCorp CEO, Yugi's rival, brilliant duelist obsessed with proving himself the best, signature card Blue-Eyes White Dragon (owns all copies he can acquire). Initially antagonistic, becomes a recurring rival/ally. Already an in-game character.
- **Joey Wheeler (Katsuya Jonouchi)** — Yugi's best friend, scrappy underdog duelist, competitive and hot-headed but fiercely loyal.
- **Téa Gardner (Anzu Mazaki)** — Yugi's close friend, the group's emotional/moral center, less focused on dueling herself than supporting the others. Already an in-game character.
- **Tristan Taylor (Hiroto Honda)** — another of Yugi's core friend group, comic relief, protective of the group.
- **Solomon Muto** — Yugi's grandfather, owner of the Kame Game Shop (already reflected as "Kame Game Shop" in this game's Domino City), a veteran duelist and game historian himself.
- **Mokuba Kaiba** — Seto's younger brother, fiercely loyal to Seto, occasionally a target for villains trying to pressure Kaiba.
- **Maximillion Pegasus** — eccentric creator of Duel Monsters, wields the Millennium Eye (mind-reading), hosts the Duelist Kingdom tournament, initial major antagonist.
- **Mai Valentine** — confident, sharp-tongued tournament duelist, recurring rival-turned-ally to the group.
- **Rebecca Hawkins** — a young American duelist prodigy and rival to Yugi/Solomon.
- **Bakura Ryou** — gentle, mild-mannered friend of the group who possesses the Millennium Ring; his body is periodically taken over by the malevolent spirit within it (Yami Bakura), a recurring antagonist force separate from Bakura's own will.
- **Marik Ishtar** — tomb-keeper-clan heir and major antagonist during the Battle City arc, wields the Millennium Rod, has his own darker alternate persona.
- **Odion (Rishid)** — Marik's loyal adopted brother/protector, willing to impersonate Marik to protect him.
- **Shadi** — a mysterious tomb-keeper who appears periodically to test/judge those who hold Millennium Items, more a recurring mystical figure than a story regular.

## Major arcs (Yugi-protagonist era only)

- **Duelist Kingdom**: Pegasus's island tournament, using stolen "soul" leverage over Yugi's grandfather to force Yugi's entry - the series' first big tournament arc.
- **Battle City**: a Domino City-wide tournament organized by Kaiba, introduces Marik/Rishid/the Egyptian God cards, structured as a rank-up-through-duels city tournament (a strong structural template for this game's "King of Games"/tournament-pass systems).
- **Waking the Dragons / Orichalcos** (anime-original arc): a mystical secondary antagonist arc where duelists risk losing their souls to a rival power (the Orichalcos), even further raising the "duels have real stakes" tone.
- **Ceremonial Battle / Memory World**: the endgame arc resolving the Pharaoh's amnesia and his true identity/backstory in ancient Egypt, culminating in a final duel between Yugi and the Pharaoh to let him move on.

## Suggested mapping onto the existing world3/world4 split

World-builder's earlier review flagged that world3 ("King of Games") and world4 ("Domino City") both independently implement Yugi/Kaiba/Téa as unsynced, separate character states (marrying Téa in one has no effect in the other) - that's a design call for the user, not something to auto-resolve. This reference is meant to ground *both* worlds' portrayals in the same canon regardless of which way that gets resolved: Solomon's Kame Game Shop, KaibaCorp, Battle-City-style tournament structure, and the Millennium Item/Shadow Game mystique are all fair game for either or both worlds to draw from.
