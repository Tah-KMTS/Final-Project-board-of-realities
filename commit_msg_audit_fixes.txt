Audit Fix: Water Tiles, City Terrain, Landmark Overlays & Route-Aware Swimming

Comprehensive audit of all requested features revealed five gaps. This
commit closes them all:

WATER TILES ON MAP
- Added WATER_ROWS = [1, 2, 3] to OverworldScene.js so the top edge of
  every city map is now a real 3-row coastal sea channel drawn with
  drawWaterTile() — the body of water the player must swim across to reach
  the inter-city highway
- Added a second vertical expressway at column 33 (FINANCE_V_STREETS),
  matching the real Tokaido Highway alignment

CITY-SPECIFIC ENVIRONMENT SCATTER (replaces old scatterTrees)
- New scatterEnvironment() function dispatches biome-appropriate props:
  - Tokyo / Osaka (urban marble): sparse rocks only (25% density), no
    nature — matches Sleek Luxury Architectural aesthetic
  - Kyoto (HD-2D JRPG cobblestone): cherry-blossom flowers dominant (65%)
    + rocks — matches Neo-Retro JRPG aesthetic
  - Default / Sapporo (grass biome): full mix of trees, flowers, rocks
- Scatter loop now starts at row 4, skipping the water channel rows

CITY LANDMARK BUILDING OVERLAYS (Phaser-rendered)
- New drawCityLandmarkOverlay() method renders directly in the Phaser scene:
  - Tokyo: amber-gold (#f59e0b) border stroke + translucent gold roof cap
    on the first 3 buildings — matches Option 3 Luxury Architectural style
  - Kyoto: red (#dc2626) pagoda eave header + gold finial spire on the
    first 3 buildings — matches Option 2 HD-2D JRPG style
- Region label now shows city-specific names: "Tokyo — Luxury Financial
  District", "Kyoto — Shinto Pagoda District", etc.

INTER-CITY HIGHWAY ROUTE WIRED TO SWIM BUTTON
- WorldScreen.jsx now imports getRouteByCities() from interCityHighways.js
- The "Swim Water Channel" button derives the next city destination and
  looks up the real route from INTER_CITY_ROUTES; statusMsg now shows
  route name, water bodies, and landmark waypoints (e.g. "Tokaido Highway
  & Hakone Pass | Crossing: Lake Biwa Channel | Heading to: Hakone
  Mountain Pass → Fuji Summit Viewpoint")

COASTAL SEA CHANNEL IN-WORLD LABEL
- Phaser text label "〰 COASTAL SEA CHANNEL — SWIM TO CROSS 〰" placed at
  the centre of the water zone so players understand what the water body is
